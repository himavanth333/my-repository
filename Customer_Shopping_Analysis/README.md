## Customer Shopping Behavior & Sales Analysis

## Project Overview:
Analyzed 99,457 customer transaction records to understand purchasing behavior, customer demographics, product-category performance, payment preferences, and revenue trends using MySQL and Microsoft Power BI.

## Objectives:
- Analyze customer behavior by gender and age.
- Identify high-performing product categories.
- Evaluate payment-method preferences.
- Compare revenue across shopping malls.
- Generate actionable business insights.

## Tools & Technologies
- **MySQL Workbench:** Data validation and SQL analysis.
- **Microsoft Power BI:** Interactive dashboard and data visualization.

## Key Insights
- Female customers generated higher total revenue.
- Customers aged 56+ contributed the highest revenue.
- Clothing was the leading category by revenue.
- Cash was the most frequently used payment method.
- Technology generated substantial revenue despite fewer transactions.
- Revenue varied across shopping malls.
